🌾 Plotbound
A single-player roguelike deck-building card game about farming. Clear 100+ hand-tuned stages, master 3 simple combos, fulfill orders, and grow your legendary farmstead — one season at a time.
Why "Plotbound"? The name evokes both literal plots of farmland and a story-bound journey of seasons. It's a fresh title (verified — no competing games), short and memorable, distinct from "Harvest" / "Farm" / "Sprout" overload, and visually grounded in the gameplay's plot-slot mechanic.
🆕 v2.1 — What's new since v2.0

  • Order System: Promoted to a top-level mechanic. 7 objective types, deterministic flavor copy, fixed per stage. See Order System.
  • Result Screen: New star rating banner, first-clear chest vs. replay reward, "Next Stage" CTA when ≥ 1 star earned.
  • Free Play preserved: Random 5-of-3 order draft remains as a separate entry point.

🎮 High Concept

Plotbound is a cozy yet strategic roguelike deck-builder where each run is a single farming season. Players draw seed and tool cards from their growing deck to fill plot slots, grow crops, and sell harvests for coins. Three simple universal combos drive every harvest decision. Equipped Perks layer additional strategy. Every season ends at the market, and every coin earned upgrades the permanent farmstead.

🎯 Target Audience
Core: Casual Deck-Builder Fans

Secondary: Cozy Mobile Gamers

Design Philosophy
🏛️ Design Pillars
Five core principles that every gameplay decision must answer to.
🌱 1. Simple to Learn, Deep to Master

Three universal combos are taught in the first 60 seconds of tutorial. Every other strategic depth comes from Perks, deck composition, and timing. First session feels natural; hundredth session reveals new Perk synergies.

Test: A 10-year-old can name all 3 combos after one tutorial run.
⏱️ 2. Respect Player Time

A complete season takes 8–15 minutes. Every round must feel purposeful. No idle waiting. Interruption possible at any round boundary.

Design rule: If a mechanic can't produce a decision in under 5 seconds, simplify it.
🎬 3. Cards Feel Alive

Every card movement — drawing, hovering, playing, discarding, reordering — has weight, momentum, and personality. Cards bend, bounce, glow, settle. Animation is not decoration; it is feedback.

Hard rule: No card ever teleports. Every transform is a tweened motion at 60fps.
📦 4. Built For Expansion

Every system is modular. New crop families, new Perks, new seasonal events — all added without touching core loop. Quarterly content drops keep engagement peaks. The 3-combo backbone never changes.

💸 5. Fair-Tilted Monetization

Cosmetics are pure choice. Perk packs are gated behind Battle Pass and direct purchase — they ARE pay-to-win, but limited by equip slots so only 2–4 are active in a session. Free players still earn Perks slowly; paying players accelerate. PvP matchmaking accounts for Perk loadout strength.

Hard rules: No energy walls during sessions. No paywalled core gameplay. Any Perk can be earned through play given sufficient time. Matchmaking ensures pay-to-win does not crush new players.

v2.1 · Main Mode
🪜 Stage Progression
100+ hand-tuned stages replace the previous "infinite free run" as the default flow. Each stage has a fixed set of orders, a difficulty band, and a 1/2/3-star clear rating that gates rewards and unlocks the next stage.
Why stages? A stable progression curve gives players a goal to work toward and a reason to upgrade specific cards. Fixed orders per stage make community guides possible (stage N always asks for the same goals), while seeded RNG keeps in-run variance for combos and draft picks.

📦 Order System
Orders are mid-run objectives that drive the player's gameplay decisions. In stage mode they're fixed per stage; in Free Play, players draft 3 from a 5-card offer. Each order pays a difficulty-scaled bonus on completion.

💰 Pricing & Penalty
  • Crops that match an active order sell at full price and "fly" to the order to advance the counter.
  • Crops that don't match any active order sell at half price.
  • Fulfilling an order pays its bonus (60 / 140 / 280 coins) and removes it from the active list — subsequent same-crop harvests revert to half price unless another order accepts it.
  • If zero orders are fulfilled at season end, the run is a Fail and final coins are reduced to 20%.
  • Partial fulfillment now keeps full earnings (v2.1) — the stage system uses stars instead of a hard fail to grade partial completion.
📝 Flavor System

Every order ships with a 1–2 sentence in-world story ("Mama Lina's pizzeria ran out of sauce mid-shift…") plus a snarky one-liner remark from the requester. Crop-specific orders pull from per-crop flavor pools (carrot, lettuce, corn, tomato, pumpkin, wheat have hand-written casts); generic types use general flavor pools (Wholesale Buyer, Famine Relief Drive, Royal Banquet, etc.). Flavor is rolled deterministically from the stage seed so repeat clears stay consistent.

🖥️ Desktop / "full" layout

Large parchment card (220×320). Stacked content: difficulty banner, big icon, title, flavor paragraph, snarky remark, mechanical objective, progress bar with counter, reward callout. Hover or tap opens a tooltip with all of the above plus reward + difficulty pill.

⚠️ Replaces Old Combo System
✨ The 3 Core Combos
Three universal combo rules form the entire bonus system. Taught in the first 60 seconds of tutorial. Universal across all crops, instantly understandable, easy to remember.
Why 3 combos? Cognitive load. Players hold 3 rules in working memory effortlessly. Every harvest decision asks two questions: "Can I stack the same crop for a bigger bonus? Can I keep the same crop streak alive from last round?" Those two instincts — quantity and loyalty — are the game's strategic spine. Combo 2 rewards the contrarian who diversifies.
🌽 Combo 1 — Abundance Bonus
Harvest 2 or more of the same crop type in the same round. The more you stack, the bigger the percentage bonus on every one of those crops.
2 same → +10%  |  3 same → +20%  |  4 same → +35%
5 same → +50%  |  6+ same → +70%
Example: Four carrots harvest together → each carrot sells for +35% (8c → ~11c). Plan plots so crops of one type ripen on the same round.
🥕🥕🥕🥕
🌈 Combo 2 — Garden Variety
Harvest 3 different crop types in the same round.
+10 flat coins on top of all 3 sales
Example: A carrot, a corn, and a tomato all mature → +10 coins added to whatever they sell for.
🥕🌽🍅
🔁 Combo 3 — Loyal Harvest
Keep harvesting only one crop type each round to build a compounding sell bonus — but only if every harvest that round is that single type. Mix in a different crop type and the streak breaks immediately, resetting the bonus to 0.
Harvest 1 crop type only → next round that type sells for +10%
Stack the streak: +10% per consecutive single-type round, max +50%
⚠️ Harvest any different type in a round → bonus resets to 0
Example: Harvest only carrots round 4 (+10%), only carrots round 5 (+20%), only carrots round 6 (+30%). Slip a tomato in on round 7 — bonus resets to 0 next round.
🔁🥕📈
⚠️ Combos 1 & 3 Are Now Opposites — Choose Your Strategy

Combos 1 and 3 create a meaningful strategic tension. Combo 1 (Abundance Bonus) rewards harvesting as many of the same crop in one round as possible. Combo 3 (Loyal Harvest) rewards harvesting only one type across consecutive rounds. Combo 2 (Garden Variety) rewards the opposite — mixing types. You can't max all three simultaneously: plan your plots accordingly.

Worked example — Abundance path: Player plants 5 carrot plots to ripen on round 6.
  • Abundance Bonus: 5 carrots → +50% on all 5 (8c × 1.5 = 12c each = 60c vs 40c base)
  • Round 5 was also carrot-only → Loyal Harvest streak is at +20% ALSO active
  • Combined: 8c × 1.5 (abundance) × 1.2 (loyal) = 14.4c per carrot → 72c for 5 carrots
  • Note: Garden Variety does NOT trigger — only one type this round
Worked example — Variety path: Player harvests 1 carrot + 1 corn + 1 tomato (3 types) on round 6.
  • Garden Variety: 3 different types → +10 flat coins
  • Abundance Bonus: only 1 of each — does NOT trigger (need ≥2 same)
  • Loyal Harvest: multiple types this round → bonus resets to 0 next round
  • Net: base sale prices + 10 coins. Good when crops ripen at different times.

⚖️ Balance Notes — How the 3 Combos Interact
ComboTriggerMax BonusCounter-play
🌽 Abundance Bonus ≥2 same crop in 1 round +70% at 6+ same crop Requires large plot count; slow crops sacrifice timing flexibility
🌈 Garden Variety ≥3 different types in 1 round +10 flat coins Resets Loyal Harvest streak; best with mixed-speed crop deck
🔁 Loyal Harvest 1 type only harvested, every round +50% at round 5 streak Breaks on any off-type crop; very vulnerable to mixed event draws
Combos 1 + 3 can stack: if you harvest only carrots this round AND there were ≥2 of them, both Abundance Bonus and Loyal Harvest apply multiplicatively. E.g., 4 carrots + ongoing streak: +35% (abundance) × 1.30 (loyal) = roughly +75.5% effective bonus — a powerful but demanding line to execute.

Combo 2 always breaks Combo 3: Garden Variety requires different types, which immediately resets the Loyal Harvest streak. Players must consciously choose between these two each round.
Perks layer on top. Some Perks modify these 3 combos (e.g., Abundance Bonus starts at 1 crop instead of 2; Loyal Harvest cap raised to 70%; Garden Variety fires with only 2 types). Some Perks add their own bonus goals. The 3 universal combos remain the foundation; Perks are the strategic specialisation.

💎 Perks — 20 Total
Perks are equipped before each season begins as bonus goals or combo modifiers. They are the game's primary pay-to-win mechanic — but balanced by limited equip slots and obtainable through play.
⚙️ How Perks Work
  • Equipped before run starts (cannot be changed mid-run)
  • Default 2 perk slots; upgradeable to 4 slots via meta-progression
  • Perks act as bonus goals (e.g., "harvest 8 carrots") OR passive modifiers (e.g., "Abundance Bonus fires at 1 crop instead of 2", "Loyal Harvest cap raised to +70%")
  • Each perk shows clear in-game progress tracking during the run
  • Perks have rarity: Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary
  • Higher rarity = stronger effect (and harder to obtain)

Pay-to-win, but balanced. Perks measurably improve run economy. We acknowledge this. Mitigations: (1) only 2–4 perks active per run, (2) PvP matchmaking accounts for total perk power, (3) all perks earnable via Perk Shards, (4) higher-rarity perks have specific situational requirements that weaker perks can also satisfy. The strategy lies in which perks to slot, not just which perks you own.

Game Feel
🎬 Card Animation Spec
Cards must feel alive. Every transformation has weight, momentum, anticipation, follow-through. This is non-negotiable production-grade craft.
Hard rule: No card ever teleports. Every position change, scale change, rotation change happens via tweened animation at 60fps. Animations are interruptible — players never wait for an animation to use the next card.
🎭 The 7 Animation Principles for Plotbound
📥
1. The Draw Arc
Cards rise from deck pile in a parabolic arc. duration: 280ms, easing: cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1) (overshoot bounce). Card faces flip mid-flight at peak. New card joins existing hand with subtle re-fan animation.
🤚
2. Hand Fanning
Hand cards arrange in slight arc. Each card rotates -3° to +3° based on position. Cards subtly shift on hover. spring: stiffness 300, damping 25. Hover lifts the card 18px, scales 1.08x, removes rotation.
👆
3. Pickup & Drag Physics
When dragged, card lifts to z=10 with a gentle scale (1.05x). Card has subtle tilt based on drag velocity (max ±15°). Drop shadow elongates. Drag has rubber-band resistance when dragging far from cursor. RAF-based motion
🎯
4. Drop & Settle
Released card travels to target slot with momentum carryover. duration: 320ms, easing: cubic-bezier(0.22, 1.20, 0.36, 1) (anticipation + overshoot). Lands with 1.1× scale spike, settles to 1.0× over 120ms. Soft "thunk" haptic + sound.
🔄
5. Reorder Cascade
When hand reorders, every card slides smoothly using FLIP technique. Cards animate in sequence with 30ms stagger to feel organic. No card jumps instantly to new position.
💨
6. Discard Fade
Discarded card flies to discard pile with curving path. Rotates 180° during flight. Fades opacity 1→0.7 over the trajectory. Lands in discard pile with -3° final rotation. duration: 380ms
⚡
7. Trigger / Combo Pop
When combo triggers, all 3 contributing cards jolt upward (60ms), then sparkle particles emit. Cards pulse golden glow. "+50%" text floats up with elastic scale. Other cards in hand subtly lean back as if reacting. duration: 800ms total

🖼️ G. Art Zone Style Guide — Crop & Tool Sprites

Crop Sprite Rules

  • Size: 192×110px canvas, sprite fills 70–80% of zone
  • Face rule: All crops have a small expressive face (eyes + optional mouth). This signals life and makes them memorable.
  • Angle: Slightly off-centre, 5–10° tilt to the right. Never perfectly vertical — feels rigid.
  • Shadow: Soft 4px drop shadow at 45°, 40% opacity. Lifts the sprite off the background.
  • Outline: 2–3px black outline, friendly not harsh. Same stroke weight as Hollow Knight style.
  • Palette: Max 5 colours per sprite. No gradients in the main sprite (flat fill + outline only). Gradient only allowed in glow effects.
  • Background: Art zone background matches card rarity very subtly (green-pale → purple-pale → gold-pale).
  • Animation at rest: Gentle idle breath loop — 2% vertical oscillation over 2.5 seconds.

Tool Sprite Rules

  • Style: Tools use a "work expression" — slight lean forward, handle at 30–45° angle.
  • No face: Tools are objects, not characters. Clarity over personality here.
  • Shine: Tools get a single white highlight line to indicate metal/glass/plastic material.
  • Scale: Slightly smaller than crops at 60–70% zone fill. Allows the blue background to breathe.
  • Glow on hover: A soft halo matching the tool's function colour (blue for water, gold for coins) pulses on hover.
  • Legendary glow: Rainbow Sprinkler has permanent rainbow halo animation at idle.
Art direction in one sentence: Crops feel alive and friendly. Tools feel purposeful and satisfying. Events feel unpredictable and slightly dramatic.

  1. Player initiates challenge or queues for matchmaking

🗑 Reset all progress (v2.1 — fully implemented)

Reset now performs a true full wipe of every persisted field: stage progress & stars, player level & XP, coins, gems, perk shards, cards owned, deck presets, upgrades, perks (owned and equipped), achievements, daily quests, battle pass progress, run history. Implementation: resetProfile() in src/storage/profile.ts wipes localStorage, runs the profile through withDefaults() (which seeds starter deck + starter perks + 5 deck presets), then returns the fresh Profile. The settings screen propagates it through onProfileChange so React state and (when signed in) cloud sync both pick up the wipe.

🛡️ Anti-Cheat Strategy
  • Server-side seed generation: All RNG generated on server, transmitted via signed token
  • Replay validation: Game logs every action; server replays to verify outcome
  • 5-sigma outlier flagging: Statistical detection of anomalous earnings
  • Time validation: Sessions take minimum 2 minutes (prevents bot-speedrunners)
  • Device fingerprinting: Detect duplicate accounts across same device
  • Monthly perk audit: Flag accounts using illegal perks for review
  • PvP shadow ban: Suspected cheaters quietly removed from public matchmaking
