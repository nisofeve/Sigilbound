Game Design Document v1.0
⚔ Sigilbound
A single-player roguelike deck-building card game about turn-based fantasy combat. Clear 100+ hand-tuned dungeon stages, master 3 simple combos, slay enemies and bosses, and forge your legendary stronghold — one battle at a time.
Why "Sigilbound"? The name evokes both the literal sigils (magical marks) bound to your combat slots and a knight's oath-bound journey through the dungeons. The title is verified clean for video games — no major competing IP — and pairs naturally with Plotbound for portfolio cohesion. The core in-game term "Sigil Slots" elegantly carries the slot-based combo mechanic forward from Plotbound's plot system.
⚙️ Built on the Plotbound Foundation

Sigilbound is a structural sibling of Plotbound v2.1. It reuses the same gameplay backbone — slot-based card placement, 3 universal combos, 100-stage progression, 20 perks/talents, 4×5 meta-upgrade tree, 999 achievements, identical battle pass scaffold, identical PvP framework, identical tech stack — and re-themes it as turn-based combat. The Migration from Plotbound section gives the engineering team a literal find-and-replace map plus the small set of additive systems (HP, equipment, enemy AI) needed to convert the existing build.

🎮 High Concept

Sigilbound is a tense yet readable roguelike deck-builder where each run is a single dungeon descent. Players draw Action and Tactic cards from their growing deck, place charging Actions into Sigil Slots, and time them to land devastating combo strikes. Three simple universal combos drive every battle decision. Equipped Talents and forged Equipment layer additional strategy. Enemies attack between your turns with telegraphed intent. Every stage cleared upgrades your permanent stronghold and brings you closer to the Lord of Ashes at floor 100.

Design Philosophy
🏛️ Design Pillars
Five core principles that every gameplay decision must answer to. Inherited from Plotbound and adapted for combat.
⚔️ 1. Simple to Learn, Deep to Master

Three universal combos are taught in the first 90 seconds of tutorial. Every other strategic depth comes from Talents, Equipment, deck composition, and enemy pattern reading. First session feels natural; hundredth session reveals new Talent/Gear synergies.

Test: A 12-year-old can name all 3 combos and explain enemy intent telegraphs after one tutorial run.
⏱️ 2. Respect Player Time

A complete dungeon stage takes 8–15 minutes. Every turn must feel purposeful. No idle waiting. Interruption possible at any turn boundary — the run state pauses cleanly.

Design rule: If a mechanic can't produce a decision in under 5 seconds, simplify it.
🎬 3. Cards Feel Alive

Every card movement — drawing, hovering, placing into a sigil slot, charging, striking, discarding — has weight, momentum, and personality. Cards bend, bounce, glow, settle. Animation is not decoration; it is feedback.

Hard rule: No card ever teleports. Every transform is a tweened motion at 60fps.
📦 4. Built For Expansion

Every system is modular. New enemy families, new Talents, new gear sets, new dungeon themes — all added without touching core loop. Quarterly content drops keep engagement peaks. The 3-combo backbone never changes.

💸 5. Fair-Tilted Monetization

Cosmetics are pure choice. Talent packs and high-tier Equipment are gated behind Battle Pass and direct purchase — they ARE pay-to-progress, but limited by equip slots so only 2–4 talents and 6 equipment pieces are active in a session. Free players still earn talents and gear slowly; paying players accelerate. PvP matchmaking accounts for total loadout strength.

Hard rules: No energy walls during sessions. No paywalled core gameplay or stages. Any Talent and any Equipment piece can be earned through play given sufficient time. Matchmaking ensures pay-to-win does not crush new players.

🎮 Turn Structure
1. Draw Phase
Draw cards up to hand limit (default: 5). Cards animate one at a time from deck onto the hand bar. Reaction cards may also trigger here.
2. Plan Phase
Player drags Action cards into open Sigil Slots; plays Tactic cards directly (instant effect). No turn time limit. Free target selection — drag Tactics onto enemy or self.
3. Charge Phase
All slotted Actions tick down by 1 charge on End Turn tap. Visual: charge bars deplete with a satisfying "wind-up" animation.
4. Resolution Phase
Mature Actions (charge = 0) fire in slot order. Core combos check runs here. Combo bonuses apply. Damage flies to targets, status effects apply, slot clears for the next Action.
5. Enemy Turn
Each living enemy executes its telegraphed intent shown last turn (attack, defend, summon, debuff). Player HP reduces if hit lands. Defense reduces incoming damage.
6. Status Tick
DoTs (burn, bleed) apply. HoTs (regen) apply. Buffs/debuffs decrement. Enemy intents update for next turn — telegraphs visible to player.

100+ hand-tuned dungeon stages form the default flow. Each stage has a fixed enemy roster, a difficulty band, and a 1/2/3-star clear rating that gates rewards and unlocks the next stage. Mirrors Plotbound's stage system 1:1.

👹 Enemy System
Enemies replace Plotbound's "order cards" as the run-defining presence. In stage mode they're fixed per stage; in Free Skirmish, players draft from a randomised encounter pool. Each enemy has HP, an attack pattern, telegraphed intents, and a difficulty-scaled gold drop on defeat.
🩸 Enemy Card Anatomy

Every enemy is rendered as a small portrait card on the battlefield (mirroring how Plotbound renders order cards). Each enemy card displays:

  • Sprite + name plate (top): chunky silhouette, name in Cinzel, type pill
  • HP bar: red-to-gold gradient, current/max numeric overlay, tweens smoothly on damage
  • Intent telegraph (under HP): icon + number showing what the enemy will do next turn — e.g. ⚔ 12 (will attack for 12), 🛡 8 (will block 8), 💀 (will summon), ⏳ (charging multi-turn ability)

Strategic Spine — Direct Rename of Plotbound Combos
✨ The 3 Core Combos
Three universal combo rules form the entire damage-bonus system. Taught in the first 90 seconds of tutorial. Universal across all damage types, instantly understandable, easy to remember. Mathematically identical to Plotbound's three combos — just re-themed: replace "crop type" with "damage type" and "harvest" with "resolve".
Why 3 combos? Cognitive load. Players hold 3 rules in working memory effortlessly. Every Resolution Phase asks two questions: "Can I stack the same damage type for a bigger bonus? Can I keep my single-type streak alive from last turn?" Those two instincts — focus and discipline — are the game's strategic spine. Combo 2 rewards the contrarian who diversifies.
⚔️ Combo 1 — Onslaught
Resolve 2 or more Actions of the same damage type in the same Resolution Phase. The more you stack, the bigger the percentage bonus on every one of those Actions.
2 same → +10%  |  3 same → +20%  |  4 same → +35%
5 same → +50%  |  6+ same → +70%
Example: Three Steel Actions resolve together → each one deals +20% damage (18 → 21.6 each, 65 total instead of 54). Plan your slot timings so multiple same-type Actions ripen together.
⚔️⚔️⚔️
🌈 Combo 2 — Triadic Strike
Resolve 3 different damage types in the same Resolution Phase.
+10 flat damage on top of all 3 strikes + draw 1 next turn
Example: A Steel slash, a Pyre fireball, and a Frost shard all resolve → each gets +10 flat damage AND you draw +1 card next turn. Best with mixed-charge-time decks.
⚔️🔥❄️
🔁 Combo 3 — Relentless
Resolve only one damage type each turn to build a compounding damage bonus — but only if every Action that turn is that single type. Mix in a different type and the streak breaks immediately, resetting the bonus to 0.
Resolve 1 type only → next turn that type deals +10%
Stack the streak: +10% per consecutive single-type turn, max +50%
⚠️ Resolve any different type in a turn → bonus resets to 0
Example: Resolve only Pyre on turn 4 (+10%), only Pyre turn 5 (+20%), only Pyre turn 6 (+30%). Slip a Steel hit in on turn 7 — bonus resets to 0 next turn.
🔁🔥📈
⚠️ Combos 1 & 3 Are Now Opposites — Choose Your Strategy

Combos 1 and 3 create a meaningful strategic tension. Combo 1 (Onslaught) rewards resolving as many of the same damage type in one turn as possible. Combo 3 (Relentless) rewards resolving only one type across consecutive turns. Combo 2 (Triadic Strike) rewards the opposite — mixing types. You can't max all three simultaneously: build your deck and time your slot placements accordingly.

Worked example — Onslaught + Relentless path: Player charges 5 Pyre Actions to resolve on turn 6.
  • Onslaught: 5 Pyre → +50% on all 5 (12 damage × 1.5 = 18 each = 90 total vs 60 base)
  • Turn 5 was also Pyre-only → Relentless streak is at +20% ALSO active
  • Combined: 12 × 1.5 (Onslaught) × 1.2 (Relentless) = 21.6 per Pyre → 108 total damage
  • Note: Triadic Strike does NOT trigger — only one type this turn
Worked example — Triadic Strike path: Player resolves 1 Steel + 1 Pyre + 1 Arcane (3 types) on turn 6.
  • Triadic Strike: 3 different types → +10 flat damage on each + draw 1 next turn
  • Onslaught: only 1 of each — does NOT trigger (need ≥2 same)
  • Relentless: multiple types this turn → bonus resets to 0 next turn
  • Net: base damage + 30 flat + tempo from extra card. Strong when slot timings are forced apart.

Combat Mechanics — REPLACES Plotbound Plot Slots
🔯 Sigil Slots & Combat
The slot mechanic is the gameplay's heart. Sigil Slots are inscribed magical ranks where Action cards bind, charge, and unleash. Identical placement, charge, and resolve mechanics to Plotbound's plot system — re-themed and aimed at enemies instead of selling crops.
⚙️ How a Slot Works
  1. Bind: Drag an Action from hand into an empty slot. Card snaps in, glows in its damage type colour.
  2. Charge: Each turn the player ends, all bound Sigils tick down by 1. A radial charge ring fills clockwise.
  3. Resolve: When charge reaches 0, the Action fires at start of Resolution Phase. Slots resolve left to right. Damage popups float up from each enemy.
  4. Combo Check: After all slots resolve, the engine evaluates Onslaught (count same types), Triadic Strike (count distinct types), and Relentless streak (compare to last turn's set).
  5. Clear: Resolved Action moves to discard. Slot is empty and bind-ready next turn.
🎯 Targeting Rules
  • By default, Actions auto-target the leftmost living enemy when bound
  • Target can be reassigned by tapping the Sigil → tap an enemy
  • AoE Actions hit all enemies (or N adjacent) — no manual target needed
  • Enemy death during resolution: remaining Actions targeting the dead enemy cascade to the next leftmost living enemy
  • If no enemies remain mid-resolution, remaining Actions are wasted (still count toward combo / objectives)
🛡️ Block & Defense

Damage taken is reduced by Block, then Defense, then dealt to HP:

damage_to_hp = max(0, raw_damage - block - defense)
  • Block resets at end of player turn (must reapply each turn)
  • Defense is a permanent stat from Equipment + Talents (always active)
  • Block above damage: excess Block discarded (doesn't carry over)
  • Some enemies pierce Block (telegraph icon: 🗡 piercing)

🔨 Crafting & Upgrading
  • Soul Shard cost to craft: Common 50, Uncommon 100, Rare 250, Epic 500, Legendary 1,000 — identical scale to Plotbound's Perk Shard pricing.
  • Upgrade tiers: Each piece can be upgraded +1 to +5 using gold (50 → 200 → 600 → 1500 → 4000) and 1-3 dust shards. Upgraded gear has higher numeric stats but the same special ability — keeps balance predictable.

🎬 Card Animation Spec

Same animation philosophy as Plotbound. The "no card teleports" principle is non-negotiable.

Direct port: All 7 of Plotbound's animation principles transfer 1:1 to Sigilbound. Only the destination targets change (cards travel to enemies instead of plot slots).
⚖️ The 7 Animation Principles
  • 1. No teleports. Every card movement is tweened. If a card needs to go from hand to discard pile, it flies — never disappears and reappears.
  • 2. 60fps target. All tweens run at 60fps on mid-range mobile (iPhone SE 2020 baseline). GPU-only transforms.
  • 3. Easing curves matter. Use ease-out-back for impact (cards landing on enemy), ease-in-quart for departures (cards leaving hand).
  • 4. Anticipation before impact. Card hovers slightly above target for 80ms before impact tween completes — gives the brain time to register what's hitting.
  • 5. Damage numbers float. Numbers spawn at impact point, float +60px, fade over 600ms. Crits use larger font + screen shake.
  • 6. Combo trigger = cascade. When a combo fires, a golden shockwave radiates from the sigil row, then each contributing slot pulses in sequence (200ms stagger). Mirrors Plotbound's "harvest cascade".
  • 7. End-of-turn discard fan. Cards in hand fan-flip to discard pile in a clockwise arc, 50ms stagger between each. Even if invisible, the engine animates them.

⚔️ 1v1 Duel Mode — Async PvP

Plotbound parity: Players don't fight live. Both players are given the same enemy seed (same enemy types, same intents, same RNG), and whoever clears it with higher score wins. This avoids netcode complexity while feeling competitive.
🏛️ Match Flow
  1. Queue: Player taps "Duel" → matchmaker pairs by Sigil Rating + Power Score
  2. Seed assigned: Both players receive identical 5-stage gauntlet (server seed)
  3. Player 1 plays their gauntlet, results recorded
  4. Player 2 plays the same gauntlet within 24h
  5. Score compared — winner determined by: total stars → HP remaining → fewer turns → fewer cards used
  6. Both players see replay of the other's run on the result screen
  7. Sigil Rating updates ELO-style

🎯 Matchmaking Power Score (anti-pay-to-win)

PowerScore = (TalentPower × 1.0) + (EquipmentPower × 0.6)

  • Talent Power = sum of rarity weights (Common 1, Uncommon 2, Rare 4, Epic 8, Legendary 16)
  • Equipment Power = sum across 6 slots (Common 1, …, Mythic 32)
  • Players matched within ±15% Power Score, then within ±200 Sigil Rating
  • Whales match whales. Free players match free players.
