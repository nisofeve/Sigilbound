Section III · The Game

Build a deck. Bind your cards to slots. Win the round.

Each run begins with a curated starting deck and a chosen Talent. The player draws a hand of Action and Tactic cards, binds them to one of three enemy slots, and ends the turn. Damage resolves with rich animations, status effects layer on top of one another, and the run advances stage by stage.

The slot-bind mechanic gives every card a positional read — placement matters as much as cost — and produces the kind of tense, one-more-turn loop that defines great roguelikes.

Section XII · The Technical Moat

Built once. Runs everywhere. Cheats nowhere.

The engine is a pure-TypeScript module that runs byte-identically on the client and inside Firebase Cloud Functions. Every run is signed at start, validated at submit. The same code that powers a player's hand on their phone validates that same hand on the server. This is the architectural backbone for cloud leaderboards, async PvP, and an extremely low marginal cost to scale.
